Active Exts is our extensions and tools pack for Clickteam Fusion 2.5.
This pack contains convenient and optimized extensions/tools that will
improve your develompent expirience with Fusion.

Active Exts is still in development, and will be updated in the future,
as well as expanded with new extensions and tools.
Feel free to suggest your extension ideas, fixes, and improvements to our Email, or via other methods.
Extensions:
Trigger
A simple, yet convenient extension to make invisible triggers, interaction zones, collisions etc.
Uses little memory and doesn't clutter the image bank (unlike, for example, an invisible Active).
Has some nice features to display in the editor.

Build Tweaker (WIP)
Build Tweaker for your Fusion applications.
Cleans up all unnecessary data in EXE, DLL, MFX, MVX, IFT, and SFT files, resulting in a smaller
build size, a bit faster loads, while also making it impossible to load Runtime MFXs into the editor,
if they originally contained Edittime data.
Gives you better control over application's metadata, such as icons, and other details.
Currently in development.
